THE URBAN NEST STORY

Urban Nest was established with a singular vision: to refine how people experience home in Coimbatore. We believe that furniture is not just functional wood and fabric—it is the backdrop of your daily life, your family gatherings, and your personal sanctuary.

We specialize in contemporary furniture curation and bespoke tailoring. Our comprehensive range spans plush modular sectionals, solid Grade-A teak dining sets, luxurious bedroom cots, and handcrafted indoor decor swings.

Whether you are furnishing a new apartment or revitalizing a heritage villa, our approach to modern home styling ensures every piece is proportionally balanced, deeply comfortable, and built to endure generations.
